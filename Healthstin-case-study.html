Overview

Healthstin is an Australia-based healthcare provider specializing in physiotherapy, exercise physiology, and allied health services. With a growing digital presence, Healthstin aimed to attract more local patients through organic search and convert that traffic into booked appointments across multiple service pages.


The Challenge

Healthstin faced limited organic visibility in a competitive local healthcare market. While the website had multiple service pages, rankings for high-intent keywords like “physio near me” were not fully optimized, resulting in missed traffic and lead opportunities.


Additionally, inconsistent content structure and underutilized SEO strategies led to slow traffic growth and underperformance across key landing pages.


Core Challenges

The Solution

A structured, local-focused SEO strategy was implemented to improve rankings, traffic, and overall search presence.

  1. Technical SEO & Site Structure Optimization: Conducted a full technical audit to improve crawlability, fix indexing issues, and enhance page speed. I also restructured key service pages to ensure clear internal linking and better alignment with search intent, especially for local queries.
  2. Local SEO & Keyword Targeting: I implemented a local SEO strategy focused on high-volume, intent-driven keywords such as “physio near me” (16K monthly searches). This included optimizing on-page elements, creating geo-targeted pages, and improving Google Business signals to dominate local search results.
  3. Content Expansion & Page Optimization: I optimized existing service pages and expanded content to better answer user queries and improve engagement. High-performing pages like the “best physio near me” page were enhanced to capture more traffic and convert visitors into leads.

The Results

  • Total Organic Traffic: Reached 1.9K monthly visitors, showing strong upward growth
  • Top Page Performance: “Best physio near me” page generated 1,214 visits (+63.8%) alone
  • Keyword Growth: Individual pages ranking for 25–62 keywords, significantly improving visibility
  • Search Rankings: Achieved Top 5 ranking for high-volume keyword “physio near me” (16K volume)
  • Page Expansion: Indexed pages increased to 106 pages, supporting broader keyword coverage
  • Estimated Revenue Impact: Generated $2.7K+ value from organic traffic on a single page

The Outcome

Healthstin successfully transformed its organic search presence into a high-performing acquisition channel. By focusing on local SEO, targeted content, and technical improvements, the brand now ranks competitively for high-volume keywords and consistently attracts qualified traffic.


Key Wins

  • Local SEO is a major growth driver for service-based businesses
  • Optimizing high-intent pages delivers outsized results (1 page drove 60%+ traffic)
  • Keyword targeting + content depth = higher rankings and conversions
  • Technical SEO lays the foundation for scalable organic growth
  • Consistent optimization compounds traffic gains over time
